FAQ

Is Employment Agency Singapore a licensed employment agency in Singapore?

Yes. We are an employment agency licensed by the Ministry of Manpower, licence number [licence number].

Which countries can you source cleaners and construction workers from?

Cleaners from Malaysia, Myanmar and PRC (China). Construction workers from Bangladesh, India and PRC. We'll advise which countries fit your sector and quota.

How long does it take to hire a foreign worker?

Lead time depends on the source country and the Work Permit approval process.

How much does an employment agency charge in Singapore?

Our placement fee starts from S$1,200 per worker. Government charges such as levy, security bond and insurance are paid at cost. See our fee table for details.

Can you supply cleaners on a contract basis?

Yes. With manpower outsourcing, cleaners stay on our payroll and we deploy them to your site, covering levy and insurance.

Do you handle Work Permit applications and renewals?

Yes. We handle IPA applications, medical check-up bookings, issuance, renewals and cancellations, including for workers you found yourself.

Which employment agency is the best in Singapore?

Look for an agency that is MOM-licensed, clear about fees and experienced in your sector. Check its licence on MOM's EA Directory and read its Google reviews.

Is it better to go through a recruitment agency?

For foreign workers, usually yes. An agency handles sourcing, screening and Work Permit paperwork, which saves time and lowers the risk of a rejected application.
